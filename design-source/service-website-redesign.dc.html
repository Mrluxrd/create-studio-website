The problem

The site is not wrong. It is out of date.

Dated visual design, content added page by page over the years, a mobile experience that was never really considered, and slow pages that lose visitors before they read anything.

The instinct is to start from zero. That usually destroys value: existing content, search rankings and recognisable brand elements are worth keeping. The job is to identify what still works and rebuild around it.

Our solution

Audit first, then rebuild what matters.

We audit the current site — structure, content, mobile behaviour, speed and conversion paths — then reorganise the content architecture and apply a modern visual direction. Valuable pages, URLs and content are preserved deliberately, not by accident.

Existing hosting and domains

If you already have an active hosting account, Create Studio will review the server, platform, access level, performance, compatibility and technical requirements before confirming whether it can support the redesigned website. A new hosting charge may not be required once an existing account is reviewed and confirmed suitable.

If new hosting is required, the account will be purchased and registered under your legal name. Create Studio may configure and manage the account, but you remain its owner. An existing domain must remain registered under your name — we may assist with DNS configuration and technical management but do not assume ownership.

Client Ownership

Your domain and hosting account are purchased and registered under your legal name or business name. You retain ownership and control of both assets. Create Studio handles the approved setup, configuration and technical management while the service agreement remains active.
