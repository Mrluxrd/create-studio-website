Canonical and Open Graph URLs currently use the placeholder origin https://PRODUCTION-DOMAIN.example. Replace it everywhere once the official Create Studio domain is confirmed. Do not use the Claude preview address as a canonical domain.

Structured data limits

Only include structured data that represents visible, verified business information — organisation name, contact details, location and service descriptions that already appear on the page.

Do not add review, rating, aggregate-rating, client-count, statistic or results markup. None of it is verified, and none of it appears on the site.

Social profile URLs are not yet confirmed. Omit sameAs until Instagram and LinkedIn addresses are supplied.

Contact prefill parameters

?service=crm-custom-systems — selects CRM or Custom System, shows CRM or Custom System — Custom Quote, reveals the CRM question set, disables the budget subtotal. Used by every CRM call to action.
?package=essential | business | premium | custom-website | essential-care | growth | growth-pro | help-me-choose — website and plan calls to action only.
A CRM call to action must never pass package=custom-website. The visitor can still change the selected service after arriving.
