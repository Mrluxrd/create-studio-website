The problem

The information exists. It just lives in nine places.

Leads in one inbox, clients in a spreadsheet, calendars in someone’s phone, commissions calculated by hand at the end of the month. Nothing is wrong individually — together it costs hours every week and hides the numbers you need to make decisions.

Off-the-shelf CRMs solve part of this and force you to change how you work for the rest. A custom system does the opposite: it follows your process.

Our solution

A system shaped to your operation.

We map the real workflow first — who does what, in what order, with what information — then design and build the modules that matter, integrate what you already use, and set role-based access so each person sees what they need.

Infrastructure and ownership

If a dedicated domain, subdomain, hosting environment, cloud account, database or storage account is required, ownership and billing responsibilities are defined in the proposal.
