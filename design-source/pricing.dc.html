Client Ownership

Your domain and hosting account are purchased and registered under your legal name or business name. You retain ownership and control of both assets. Create Studio handles the approved setup, configuration and technical management while the service agreement remains active.

Frequently asked questions

Before you ask.

What is included in every website project?

Visual direction, responsive design, technical configuration, forms and calls to action, domain connection, two consolidated revision rounds and initial launch support.

How does payment work?

50% to begin the project and 50% before launch.

How long does a website take?

Typically 2–4 weeks. The project calendar begins once the initial payment and complete content have been received.

What counts as a revision round?

A consolidated set of feedback reviewed together. Two rounds are included; additional rounds are quoted separately so scope stays clear.

Can the final price change?

The final proposal may be adjusted according to scope, content and functionality. Anything outside the agreed scope is quoted before work begins — there are no hidden fees.

What about domains, licenses and subscriptions?

Domains, licenses, subscriptions and premium third-party services are quoted separately. You own them.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Monthly SEO growth and maintenance plans start at US$100/month and are optional.
