The problem

Nobody notices maintenance until something breaks.

Expired certificates, plugin updates left for months, no recent backup, a contact form quietly failing for weeks. None of it is visible from the outside until a customer cannot reach you.

Support needs also differ by site. A five-page informational site and an active platform with integrations do not require the same attention, so a single fixed plan for everyone rarely fits.

Our solution

Quiet, consistent care at the level your site needs.

We keep the technical base healthy — hosting, SSL, updates, backups, monitoring — handle minor content changes, and flag improvements worth making. Three plan levels exist so you pay for the attention your site actually requires.
