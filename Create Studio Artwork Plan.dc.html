Higgsfield artwork plan · homepage v1.0 · August 2026

One continuous visual story, generated in twelve pieces.

The approved layout is unchanged. This document specifies every artwork slot already present in that layout — ratio, export size, subject placement, negative space, crops, motion and parallax — so Higgsfield prompts can be written against exact measurements. No final artwork is produced here.

Visual family — constant across all twelve
Background: true #09090B, no vignette, no lighter panel.
Colour: #2563FF → #8B5CF6 only. No third hue, no white light, no neon.
Form: fluid open curves drawn from the monogram's unclosed C — one gesture, never a lattice.
Line: single stroke weight family, 6–14px at 2400px export.
Texture: fine even grain, 2–4% opacity, identical across every asset.
Depth: one soft focal falloff, no cast shadows on the background.
Motion: ease-in-out, no bounce, no strobe, ≤0.4 perceived speed.
Never generate
Smoked glass · glass sculpture · cubes · repeated squares · floating UI panels · data nodes · metallic machinery · generic tech imagery · robots · AI brains · cyberpunk environments · random neon · fake website screens · fake portfolio work · any readable text or numerals inside the artwork.
The interface keeps its approved cards and containers. This restriction governs generated artwork only.
Energy curve across the scroll
Hero
Concentrated. Highest gradient density of the page.
Portfolio
Receding. Artwork yields to real client work.
Services
Directional. One family, six distinct behaviours.
Process
Linear. A single path carries left to right.
Pricing
Restrained. Atmosphere only, never a subject.
Contact
Expanding. Echoes the hero, opened outward.
Gradient direction rotates clockwise by roughly 30° per section — hero reads blue-lower-left to violet-upper-right; by the contact section the same axis has travelled a half turn. Never reverse it between adjacent sections.
