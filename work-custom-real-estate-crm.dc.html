Overview

Built for how the business actually works.

Luxury real estate involves more than storing names and contact information. Advisors must coordinate buyers, sellers, listings, developers, appointments, follow-ups, opportunities, documents, referrals and transactions across extended sales cycles.

This project explores a custom CRM structured around that specific operational reality.

The challenge

Generic CRM platforms can require a business to adapt its workflow to the software.

The challenge was to define a system around the actual way a luxury real estate advisor manages relationships, active opportunities, property information, communications and next actions.

The approach

Create Studio mapped the system around the business workflow first. The experience was organized into connected modules that can support:

Clients and leads
Buyer requirements
Sellers
Properties
Developers and projects
Active opportunities
Follow-ups
Appointments and calendar activity
Documents
Referral-fee tracking
Pipeline stages
Notes and activity history

These modules belong to the approved scope of this project. They are not automatically included in every CRM Create Studio builds — each system is scoped around the functions that business actually requires.

The solution

One coherent operational environment.

The CRM creates a more centralized operational environment in which property, client and opportunity information can be managed through one coherent interface.

Clear daily priorities
Faster access to relevant information
Consistent follow-up
Better organization
Connected records
Reduced dependence on disconnected tools
Responsive access across devices
