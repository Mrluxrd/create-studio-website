Overview

Credible, established, and easy to navigate.

The Joan Leonardo website required a more contemporary digital presence capable of presenting legal experience and professional services with greater clarity.

The objective was to create a website that feels credible and established while remaining approachable and easy for prospective clients to navigate.

The challenge

Legal websites must communicate trust quickly. Dense content, unclear service organization or an outdated interface can make it difficult for visitors to understand the firm’s capabilities or identify the correct next step.

The project required a stronger hierarchy without losing the seriousness and professionalism expected from a legal practice.

The approach

Create Studio refined the website around a clearer structure and a more confident visual language.

Clear service presentation
Stronger information hierarchy
More confident typography
Focused calls to action
Responsive layouts
Easier access to professional information
A visual system aligned with the character of the practice
The solution

A cleaner, more deliberate structure.

The redesigned experience presents the practice through a cleaner and more deliberate structure.

The website helps visitors understand who Joan Leonardo is, which services are available and how to begin a professional inquiry.
